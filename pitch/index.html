How it works

One voice turn,
from keypress to ink.

01 · Listen

Push-to-talk

Your voice + one look at the screen, only while you hold the key.

02 · Think

Realtime voice model

Hears, sees and answers in one stream. Remembers the whole session and your courses.

03 · Aim

Grounding

UI tree → OCR → Moondream. Never trusts the model's raw coordinates.

04 · Draw

Tools

point · circle · underline · arrow · write · plot, traced live by Gerald's cursor.
