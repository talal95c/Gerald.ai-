Trust & privacy

A tutor on your screen, not a spy.

  • Vision on demandNo continuous capture: the screen is only read while you hold the shortcut.
  • Real consentBefore any action, an “Allow / Cancel” prompt. The model can't approve itself.
  • GuardrailsMax 8 actions per turn, Esc stops everything, moving the mouse 40 px takes back control.
  • Local firstYour courses are indexed on your machine; proactive mode only reads the window title.
