How it works

One voice turn,
from keypress to ink.

01 · Listen

Push-to-talk

Your voice + one look at the screen, only while you hold the key.

02 · Think

Realtime voice model

Hears, sees and answers in one stream. Remembers the whole session and your courses.

03 · Aim

Grounding

UI tree → OCR → Moondream. Never trusts the model's raw coordinates.

04 · Draw

Tools

point · circle · underline · arrow · write · plot, traced live by Gerald's cursor.

0.6 sto locate a target
~2.4 pxmedian pointing error
77 → 91 %pedagogy score on 16 judged scenarios
The hard part is pointing. Raw model coordinates are ~90 px off, so every gesture is anchored on the UI tree, OCR, or Moondream. And we don't guess if it teaches well: a harness scores 16 tutoring scenarios on an 8-criterion rubric.
